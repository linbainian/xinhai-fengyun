Exactly a 2-row by 3-column terrain texture atlas for a top-down pixel JRPG. Six opaque square cells with zero gutters, straight horizontal and vertical divisions, seamless repeatable floor textures only. Row 1 cells: three distinct low-noise violet-gray alien stone ground variants, small restrained cyan mineral specks and shallow purple cracks, no high rocks. Row 2 cells: three distinct cool blue-gray industrial concrete floor variants with muted aggregate and subtle straight paving joints. Orthographic overhead view, uniform diffuse light, same pixel scale in all cells, deliberately crisp pixel art and restrained palette, low contrast so chibi game actors remain clear. No objects, crystals protruding above floor, trees, creatures, portals, walls, shadows, labels, text, borders, holes, cliff edges or perspective. All six cells full bleed, no magenta background, no empty margins. Distinct variants with matching edge colors in each row, made for deterministic slicing to 48-pixel game tiles.
